Thuluth Script

Thuluth: The King of Scripts

Thuluth (Arabic: ثلث, “one-third”) is perhaps the most famous of all classical Arabic scripts. Known for its elegant, cursive flow and complex sloping lines, it has been the primary script for architectural inscriptions in the Islamic world for centuries.

In the UAE, Thuluth is widely used in monumental calligraphy, such as the inscriptions found in the Sheikh Zayed Grand Mosque and various landmark government buildings. It is a demanding script that requires absolute mastery of proportion.
