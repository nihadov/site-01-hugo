Editorial Policy

To maintain the quality and reliability of this directory, we adhere to a strict set of editorial guidelines.

Inclusion Criteria

  1. UAE Base: The artist must have clear evidence of being based in the United Arab Emirates.
  2. Public Contact Path: We only list artists who have a reliable, public way to be contacted (website, official social media, or contact form).
  3. Professional Standing: We prioritize artists with a proven track record, exhibitions, or significant contributions to the field.
  4. No Scraped Content: We do not include entries from SEO directories or lead-generation spam sites.

Curated, Not Exhaustive

This is a curated reference, not an exhaustive list of every person who practices calligraphy. We focus on quality and reliability.

How to Submit Corrections

If you find an error or wish to update a listing, please use our Contact Form.
