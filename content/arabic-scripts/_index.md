# The Professional Reference for Arabic Calligraphy in the UAE.

The Dubai Calligrapher Directory is an editorial project established to document and reference the leading practitioners of Arabic calligraphy within the United Arab Emirates. 

This platform serves as a bridge for Art Directors, Cultural Institutions, and Brands to find verified, professional artists ranging from classical masters to digital innovators.
