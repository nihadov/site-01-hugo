The Dubai Calligrapher Directory is a curated, verified reference directory of UAE-based Arabic calligraphers. Our mission is to provide a reliable bridge between the public and the masters of Arabic calligraphy residing in the United Arab Emirates.

### Why This Directory Exists
Arabic calligraphy is a cornerstone of Islamic art and culture. In a digital age, finding authentic, verified, and reachable artists can be challenging. This directory serves as a central hub to:
- Promote the art of Arabic calligraphy in the UAE.
- Provide direct contact paths to professional artists.
- Ensure the authenticity and professional standing of listed calligraphers.

### How Updates Work
We regularly review our listings and accept suggestions from the community. Every entry is manually verified against our editorial policy before being published.
